How does the tool work?

The tool computes five internationally recognized financial indicators to evaluate your project's feasibility:

  • NPV (Net Present Value): The current value of all future cash flows. Positive NPV = viable project.
  • IRR (Internal Rate of Return): The annual return generated by the project. Must exceed the discount rate (cost of capital).
  • PI (Profitability Index): Return per unit of investment. Must be ≥ 1.
  • Payback Period: Time required to recover capital. Shorter is better.
  • EBITDA: Operating profit before depreciation and taxes. Reflects operational efficiency.

How to read these numbers?
  • Positive NPV: The project creates economic value — higher is better.
  • IRR > Discount Rate: Return is above the cost of capital — viable investment.
  • PI ≥ 1: Each unit of investment returns more than its current value.
  • Short Payback: Faster capital recovery = lower risk.
  • Break-even: Minimum monthly sales to cover costs (no profit or loss).
